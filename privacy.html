Privacy policy

How information is handled on this demonstration website.

Contact enquiries

This website has no enquiry forms. Calling uses your phone app. The email button opens Gmail, where you choose whether to send your message. Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry.

External services

Google Maps is embedded on this site. Google, Gmail and social platforms operate under their own privacy policies. This demo does not install analytics or advertising trackers.

Your information

To ask about information you have provided directly to the business, contact Andrew using the contact details on the homepage.

Demo status

This policy describes the website concept. The business should review and approve it against its actual practices before launch.
