FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, ring Andrew on 027 289 3799.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Marton and work across the Rangitīkei. If your job's further afield, give Andrew a ring and ask.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email with what you're planning, roughly how big it is and where it is. A few photos of the site help. Andrew will come back to you to talk it through.

Do you do the digging as well as the concrete?

Yes. Excavation and site prep go in before the pour, so the whole job can be handled together. Ask Andrew what your site needs.

What finishes can I choose from?

That depends on the job and how the concrete will be used. Ask Andrew about the options when he prices it.

How long until I can drive on a new driveway?

It depends on the mix, the weather and the job, so ask Andrew for the right timing for yours.

When can you start?

Ring Andrew to talk about timing. He'll let you know when he can fit your job in.
